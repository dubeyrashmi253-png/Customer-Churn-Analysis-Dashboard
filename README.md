# Customer-Churn-Analysis-Dashboard

**1. Project Title**

**Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard – Customer Retention Insights**

**2. Short Description**

The Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard focuses on understanding customer churn and retention patterns. It analyzes customer characteristics, tenure, product usage, and churn-related indicators to help businesses understand customer behavior.

The project transforms customer data into interactive visualizations that support the identification of customer segments with higher churn and opportunities to improve retention.

**3. Tech Stack**
 
• 📊 Power BI Desktop – Main data visualization platform used for report creation.

• 📂 Power Query – Data transformation and cleaning layer for reshaping and preparing the data.

• 🧠 DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Used for calculated measures, dynamic visuals, and conditional logic.

• 📝 Data Modeling – Relationships established among tables (resorts, snow, and data_dictionary) to enable cross-filtering and aggregation. 

**4. Data Source**

The raw dataset used for this project was acquired from Maven Analytics. The data was used to perform analysis, identify trends, evaluate key performance indicators (KPIs), and develop interactive visualizations in Power BI. 

**5. Features / Highlights**

Customer churn and retention overview. 
Churn rate monitoring. 
Analysis of customer tenure and retention patterns. 
Customer segmentation and demographic analysis. 
Exploration of churn across product categories and customer characteristics. 
Identification of customer groups with comparatively high churn. 
Interactive visualizations for exploring churn patterns. 
KPI-based reporting to summarize customer behavior.

**6. Business Problem / Solutions**

**Business Problem**

Customer churn can reduce revenue, increase customer acquisition costs, and affect long-term business growth. Without a clear understanding of customer behavior, businesses may struggle to identify customers who are more likely to discontinue their services.

**Solution:**

The Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard organizes customer information into meaningful KPIs and visualizations. It enables users to examine churn rates, compare customer segments, analyze tenure, and explore relationships between customer characteristics and churn.

The insights can help businesses investigate possible causes of churn and develop targeted customer retention strategies.

**Business Value:**

Improves visibility into customer churn and retention.

Helps identify customer segments requiring further attention.

Supports customer retention planning.

Enables data-driven customer relationship decisions.

Helps businesses monitor churn-related performance indicators.
